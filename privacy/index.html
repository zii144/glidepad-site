Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

Glidepad does not collect, store or share any personal data on servers. There is no Glidepad server.

  • Only your own devicesYour phone talks only to the computers you pair with it, directly over your own network, end-to-end encrypted.

01Who this policy covers

This policy covers the Glidepad app for iPhone and iPad, the Glidepad app for Android, and the Glidepad agent for macOS, Windows and Linux (together, “Glidepad”). Glidepad is made by Chun Yin Wong, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). The app and the agent together let you use your phone as a trackpad, keyboard, media remote and presenter for your own computer.

02Data we collect

None. Glidepad never sends us anything you do in the app or on your computer. It has no account system, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, no advertising SDK, and no tracking of any kind, and it never contacts a server run by us. We do not sell, rent or share personal data, because we do not have any.

Apple and Google run the stores you download Glidepad from. They may give developers aggregated statistics (such as download counts), and, only if you have chosen to share them with developers in your device’s settings, anonymous crash reports. Those are governed by Apple’s and Google’s own privacy policies. They do not identify you to us.

03What stays on your phone

  • A private key that identifies your phone to your computers. On iPhone and iPad it is kept in the iOS Keychain; on Android, in the Android Keystore. It is never sent over the network or to us.
  • The computers you have paired: their names, network addresses and public keys.
  • Your settings, button decks you create, and text snippets you save in Compose.

On Android, this data is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. On iPhone and iPad, it is included in your own encrypted iCloud or computer backups, like other app data, and is never readable by us.

04What travels between your phone and your computer

Glidepad works by sending input from your phone to a computer you have paired. This traffic goes directly between the two devices over your local network (or Bluetooth, on Android, if you turn that mode on). It never passes through us. Every message is authenticated and encrypted end to end (X25519 key agreement, ChaCha20-Poly1305), and pairing happens by scanning a QR code, so keys are never sent over the network.

Phone → computer

Pointer movement, clicks, scrolling and gestures; keystrokes and text you type or dictate; clipboard text when you choose to send it; photos and files you choose to send (saved to the computer’s Downloads folder).

Computer → phone

The name of the app in front, so the right controls appear; a live picture of the screen while the screen preview is open; clipboard text when you choose to fetch it; on a Mac, what is playing and the volume while the Media panel is open.

The Glidepad agent on your computer keeps its own key and the list of phones you have paired, in its settings folder. It keeps a short activity log (for example, “connected”) in memory only, and does not save it.

05Optional features that involve other companies

Dictation with the phone’s microphone

If you turn on “Dictate with This Phone” and press Dictate, your phone’s built-in speech recognition turns your speech into text: Apple’s on iPhone and iPad, Google’s on Android. Depending on your language and device, the audio may be sent to Apple or Google to be recognized, under their privacy policies (Apple, Google). Glidepad does not record or keep any audio, and the resulting text goes only to your computer.

Artwork for what is playing (Mac)

While the Media panel is open, the agent on a Mac can show the title, artist and artwork of what is playing in Music, Spotify or a YouTube tab in your browser. macOS asks your permission before the agent can query each of those apps. For Spotify and YouTube, the agent downloads the artwork image from that service’s image server (i.scdn.co or i.ytimg.com). Like any web request, this tells that service your computer’s IP address. Nothing else is sent, and nothing is sent to us.

Links in button decks

A deck button can open a website on your computer, such as YouTube. That website is opened by your computer’s browser, under that website’s own privacy policy.

06Permissions

Local Network
To find your computers on your network and connect to them.
Camera
Only to scan the pairing QR code. Images are processed on the phone and are not saved.
Photos
Only when you choose photos or videos to send to your computer. Glidepad reads only the items you pick.
Microphone and Speech Recognition
Only if you turn on dictation and press Dictate (see section 05).
Bluetooth (Android)
Only if you turn on the mode that makes the phone act as a Bluetooth mouse and keyboard.
Accessibility (macOS agent)
Required by macOS so the agent can move the pointer and type on your computer.
Screen Recording (agent, optional)
Only for the screen preview. The picture is sent to your phone and is not saved.

You can turn any of these off in your device’s settings. The features that need them will stop working; everything else keeps working.

07Keeping and deleting your data

Because everything is stored on your own devices, you control it completely:

  • On your phone: tap the “…” button next to a computer and choose “Forget This Computer”, or delete the Glidepad app to remove everything it stored.
  • On your computer: unpair a phone with glidepad-agent forget <name>, or uninstall the agent and delete its settings folder: ~/Library/Application Support/dev.Glidepad.Glidepad on macOS, %APPDATA%\Glidepad\Glidepad on Windows, or ~/.config/glidepad on Linux.

We hold no copy of any of this, so there is nothing for us to delete, export or correct on your behalf.

08Children

Glidepad does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. It has no chat, no social features and no user-generated content shared with others.

09Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act and Taiwan’s and Hong Kong’s personal data laws give you rights to access, correct, delete or move personal data held about you, and to object to its use. We do not hold personal data about Glidepad users. If you have a question or a request, contact us (see section 11) and we will reply.

10Changes to this policy

If Glidepad changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
